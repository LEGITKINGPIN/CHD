# References

The following papers from the provided corpus informed the architectural and methodological design of the Crime Hotspot Detection platform:

1. **Baqir, A., et al. (2020).** *Evaluating the Performance of Hierarchical Clustering algorithms to Detect Spatio-Temporal Crime Hot-Spots.* 3rd International Conference on Computing, Mathematics and Engineering Technologies (iCoMET).
2. **Butt, U. M., et al. (2020).** *Spatio-Temporal Crime HotSpot Detection and Prediction: A Systematic Literature Review.* IEEE Access, 8, 166553-166574.
3. **Mandalapu, V., et al. (2023).** *Crime Prediction Using Machine Learning and Deep Learning: A Systematic Review and Future Directions.* IEEE Access, 11, 60151-60170.
4. **Safat, W., et al. (2021).** *Empirical Analysis for Crime Prediction and Forecasting Using Machine Learning and Deep Learning Techniques.* IEEE Access, 9, 70086-70094.
5. **Shiraptini, T. C., et al. (2026).** *AI and Machine Learning-Enabled Cognitive Digital Twin for Crime Hotspot Detection and Analysis.* IEEE Access.
6. **Zhang, X., et al. (2020).** *Comparison of Machine Learning Algorithms for Predicting Crime Hotspots.* IEEE Access, 8, 181302-181310.
7. **Zhuang, Y., et al. (2017).** *Crime Hot Spot Forecasting: A Recurrent Model with Spatial and Temporal Information.* 

*(Note: Additional papers in the `Crime Detection using ML/` corpus were reviewed for general context regarding Botnet Detection, Weapon Detection in CCTV, and Multimodal Twitter analysis, but the above directly influenced the core spatio-temporal clustering and classification methodology of this project).*
